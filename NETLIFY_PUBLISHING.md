## Purpose
This file gives the exact Netlify publishing steps for the release-ready `MS Excel Stimulator/` package.

## Step-by-step process
1. Open `https://app.netlify.com/drop`.
2. Open the `MS Excel Stimulator/` folder on your computer.
3. Drag the **entire folder** into the Netlify Drop area.
4. Wait for Netlify to finish uploading and provisioning the URL.
5. Open the generated site URL.
6. Run the deployment testing matrix in `DEPLOYMENT.md`.
7. If you want to keep the site permanently, click **Claim your site** and connect it to a free Netlify account.

## Important checks
- Confirm `index.html` is at the root of the deployed site.
- Confirm the `icons/` folder is present.
- Confirm PWA install behavior works.
- Confirm workbook pages, enterprise console, report printing, and exports all work.

## Why Netlify is suitable here
- static-site friendly
- HTTPS by default
- easy drag-and-drop deployment
- compatible with service workers and PWA install flows
